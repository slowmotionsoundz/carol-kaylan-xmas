Day one · Wednesday

December 23 today

Land in Copenhagen, sleep by the water

    Land at CPH. Flight from Huntsville - time to add once booked. Cory is waiting at arrivals with the van; no trains, no dragging bags.

Day two · Thursday

December 24 today

Christmas Eve - kept open on purpose

  1. Morning
    Slow start. Hotel breakfast, then a short, flat stroll along Vedbæk marina if the weather is kind.
  2. Midday
    Bags ready. If Christmas dinner is in Sweden, the move happens this morning: van to Helsingør (~30 min), ferry across (20 min), on to Landskrona (~25 min). If dinner is in Denmark, nothing moves at all.
  3. Evening
    Christmas Eve dinner. Rosanna's grandmother's or her aunt's - the family decides. In Scandinavia the celebration and the gifts happen tonight.
Almost everything is closed. Shops, museums and Tivoli all shut for the 24th in both countries. The day is built around family and the table, not sights.

Day three · Friday

December 25 today

Christmas Day - the crossing is the outing

  1. Morning
    Late breakfast, no rush. Check out and roll the bags to the van.
  2. ~30 min
    Drive the coast to Helsingør. Kronborg castle - Hamlet's castle - watches over the harbor where the ferry berths.
  3. 20 min
    The ferry. The van boards with everyone in it - Cory's clip card covers the car. Twenty minutes across the strait: coffee or lunch on board, seats by the window, elevators between decks.
  4. ~25 min
    Helsingborg to Landskrona. Check in on the Swedish side, then a quiet Christmas afternoon with the family.
Meals need a plan. Christmas Day closes restaurants and shops in both countries. Eat on the ferry, at the hotel, or with family. The ferry sails every day; the exact holiday timetable gets confirmed closer to the date.

Good to know

Late December on the Øresund

Getting around

Cory drives the ClubLink van door to door the whole trip - airport pickup, every outing, airport drop-off. The only water crossing is the ferry, and the van comes aboard. The Øresund Bridge back to the airport carries a toll. No trains, no transfers, no stairs with bags.

The season and the closures

Expect 0-5°C, often wet or crisp rather than deep snow, and daylight roughly 8:45 to 15:40 - so sights belong to the morning. December 24: shops, museums and Tivoli closed in both countries. December 25: almost everything closed. December 26: shops reopen and Sweden's rea sales begin.

For Carol

Every leg is step-free - the van, the ferry elevators, the hotels. No sight needs more than a short, flat walk, and every day has a sit-down fallback. Warm layers for the ferry deck; the coffee inside is never far.

Money

Denmark uses kroner (DKK), Sweden uses kronor (SEK). Cards and phones pay for everything on both sides - no cash needed.
